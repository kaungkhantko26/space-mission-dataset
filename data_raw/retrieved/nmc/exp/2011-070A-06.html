Chemistry and Mineralogy X-Ray Diffraction/X-Ray Flourescence Instrument (CheMin)

NSSDCA ID: 2011-070A-06

Mission Name: Mars Science Laboratory (MSL)
Principal Investigator:Mr. David F. Blake

Description

The Chemistry and Mineralogy (CheMin) instrument is designed to identify and quantify the minerals present in the rocks and soil delivered by the MSL sample handling system. This will enable determination of the involvement of water in their formation, deposition, or alteration. It will also be useful in the search for potential biosignatures, energy sources for life, or indicators of past habitable environments.

CheMin is a powder X-ray diffraction instrument with X-ray flourescence capabilities. A sample is placed in one of the 74 sample cups and delivered to the CheMin instrument. An analysis run can take up to 10 hours, and the result is a simultaneous 2-D X-ray diffraction pattern and energy dispersive histogram.

For more detailed information, see:

http://msl-scicorner.jpl.nasa.gov/Instruments/CheMin/
