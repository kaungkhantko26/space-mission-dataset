NSSDCA ID: 1978-051A-14

Mission Name: Pioneer Venus Orbiter
Principal Investigator:Dr. Gerald Schubert

Description

Measurements of plasma temperatures, magnetic fields, particle composition, and other data were used to develop and test theories of atmospheric circulation and solar wind-ionosphere interactions. In the case of topography and gravity, the data (altimetry and tracking) were used both in descriptive fashion, to simply characterize the surface of Venus and its gravitational field, and in a more quantitative way to model the internal structure of the planet.
