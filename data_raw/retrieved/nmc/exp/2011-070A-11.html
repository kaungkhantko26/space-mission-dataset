Dynamic Albedo of Neutrons (DAN)

NSSDCA ID: 2011-070A-11

Mission Name: Mars Science Laboratory (MSL)
Principal Investigator:Dr. Igor G. Mitrofanov

Description

The Dynamic Albedo of Neutrons (DAN) instrument is an active/passive neutron spectrometer designed to measure the abundance and depth distribution of H- and OH- bearing materials. The science objectives are: detect and provide a quantitative estimate of the hydrogen in the subsurface throughout the mission; investigate the upper ~50 cm of the subsurface and determine the possible layering structure of hydrogen bearing materials in the subsurface; track the variability of hydrogen content in the upper regolith layer (~1 m) during the mission by periodic analysis; and track the variability of neutron radiation background (neutrons with energy <100 KeV) during the mission by periodic analysis.

DAN has an active mode that consists of inducing neutron flux from the surface with a pulsing 14 MeV neutron source and measuring its time decay (the dynamic albedo) DAN can be used during short stops of rover traverses or when the rover is parked. Short duration (<2 min) measurements can give the water-equivalent hydrogen distribution with an accuracy of ~1% by weight. Longer duration (~30 min) measurements can give an accuracy of 0.1 - 0.3% by weight.
