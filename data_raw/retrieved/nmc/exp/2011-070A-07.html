Alpha Particle X-Ray Spectrometer (APXS)

NSSDCA ID: 2011-070A-07

Mission Name: Mars Science Laboratory (MSL)
Principal Investigator:Dr. Ralf Gellert

Description

The Alpha Particle X-ray Spectrometer (APXS) is designed to determine the abundance of major elements in material on the martian surface. The APXS uses a curium-244 source to enable X-ray spectroscopy of the material. The sensor head is mounted in the robotic arm. The arm places the head typically closer than 2 cm from the target, and the emitted X-ray spectrum is measured for 15 minutes to 3 hours. Spectral resolution (FWHM) of better than 200 eV at 6.4 keV is possible below ~-5 C, and better than 150 eV below ~-15 C.

For more detailed information, see:

http://msl-scicorner.jpl.nasa.gov/Instruments/APXS/
