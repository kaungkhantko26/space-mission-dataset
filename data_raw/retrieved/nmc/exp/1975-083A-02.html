Infrared Thermal Mapper (IRTM)

NSSDCA ID: 1975-083A-02

Mission Name: Viking 2 Orbiter
Principal Investigator:Dr. Hugh H. Kieffer

Description

The purpose of the IRTM experiment was to measure the temperatures of the atmosphere and areas on the surface of Mars. The amount of sunlight reflected by the planet was also measured. The IRTM was a multichannel radiometer mounted on the orbiter's scan platform. Four small telescopes, each with seven infrared detectors, were aimed parallel to the visual imaging optical axis, and made observations every 1.12 s. The instrument was capable of measuring differences of 1 deg C throughout a temperature range of -130 C to +57 C. The field of view was circular, 5 milliradians in diameter.
