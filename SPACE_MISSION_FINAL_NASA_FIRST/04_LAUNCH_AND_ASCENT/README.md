# NASA-only launch and ascent (filtered from Workstream 1 v0.2.2)

The approved launcher scientific dataset now contains **SLS Block 1** only. Published NASA values for Falcon Heavy, Vulcan and Atlas V may be gathered independently later if the team wants those vehicles, but the manufacturer-only figures in the earlier 4-vehicle research package were omitted under the agreed NASA-first-only authoritative policy.

The SLS stage rows remain mission/revision-specific (Artemis I versus Artemis II); prelaunch profiles are not actual spacecraft telemetry. A full realistic ascent simulation is not available from the selected public inputs alone. Atmospheric table covers 0-80 km; do not apply it outside coverage silently. Pad LC-39B historical geodetic coordinate ellipsoid is Fischer 1960, not automatically WGS84; elevation is unverified.

The old Workstream 1 validation report under `historical_notes/` refers to all four originally researched rockets and **does not certify this newly filtered subset**. New filtering QA is in `../08_VALIDATION_AND_QA/`.
